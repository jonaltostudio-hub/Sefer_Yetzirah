How Gates Work: The 22 letters combine in pairs (22 × 21 ÷ 2 = 231). Each combination creates a unique "gate" or pathway of divine energy. In traditional practice, meditating on these combinations was believed to unlock creative and transformative powers. Click any two letters on the wheel to explore their gate.

About the Sefirot

Click any Sephirah to learn about its qualities and meaning. The Ten Sefirot represent the fundamental emanations through which the Infinite becomes manifest in creation.

Note: While Sefer Yetzirah originally described the ten Sefirot as the four elements plus six directions, later Kabbalah developed them into the more familiar names shown here (Keter, Chokhmah, Binah, etc.). This visualization bridges both traditions.
